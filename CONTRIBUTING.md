# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they preserve the lab's safety boundaries and reproducibility.

Please include:
- threat/risk being tested;
- exact tool and model versions;
- sanitized reproduction steps;
- before/after evidence for mitigation changes;
- mapping to OWASP LLM / MITRE ATLAS where justified;
- tests for new application-side controls.

Do not submit real secrets, personal data, proprietary prompts, or attack results from systems you do not own.
